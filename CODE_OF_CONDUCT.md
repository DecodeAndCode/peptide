# Code of Conduct

We want SuppGO to be a respectful, focused, and useful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and professional.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about problems.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Keep discussions focused on the issue or pull request.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments
- Abusive review behavior

## Reporting

Report concerns to a repository maintainer. Maintainers should review reports privately, act proportionally, and protect contributors from retaliation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues, remove participants from discussions, or restrict repository access when needed to keep collaboration healthy.
